## What Was Fixed:

### Portfolio Table Now Shows (Portfolio.jsx):

**Columns (Left to Right):**
1. **Symbol** - Stock name (e.g., TATASTEEL.NS)
2. **Quantity** - How many shares you bought (117.0000)
3. **Buy Price** - Price you bought at (₹170.06) - **CYAN COLOR**
4. **Amount Invested** - Total money spent (₹19,897.02)
5. **Current Price** - Current market price (₹169.83) - **GREEN COLOR**
6. **Market Value** - Current total value (₹19,870.11)
7. **Profit/Loss** - How much you made/lost:
   - Shows amount: **-₹26.91**
   - Shows percentage: **(-0.14%)**
   - **GREEN** if profit, **RED** if loss
8. **Action** - Close button to sell

### Example Row:
```
TATASTEEL.NS | 117.0000 | ₹170.06 | ₹19,897.02 | ₹169.83 | ₹19,870.11 | -₹26.91 (-0.14%) | [Close]
```

### Color Coding (Easy to Understand):
- **Buy Price**: Cyan (blue) - What you paid
- **Current Price**: Green - What it's worth now
- **Profit**: Green text with + sign
- **Loss**: Red text with - sign

### Summary Section:
- Total Value: Shows total worth of all stocks
- Total P&L: Shows total profit/loss

## How to Read (Simple for Anyone):

**For Each Stock:**
1. Look at **Buy Price** (cyan) - This is what you paid per share
2. Look at **Current Price** (green) - This is what it's worth now
3. Look at **Profit/Loss** (green/red) - This tells you if you're making money:
   - **Green with +** = You're making money! 🎉
   - **Red with -** = You're losing money 😔

**Example:**
- Bought SAIL at ₹128.75
- Now worth ₹129.68
- Profit: **+₹144.15 (+0.72%)** ✅ Making money!

- Bought TATASTEEL at ₹170.06
- Now worth ₹169.83
- Loss: **-₹26.91 (-0.14%)** ❌ Losing money
